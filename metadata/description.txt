You’re on the night shift at a lost property bureau. For each object, two people come forward to claim it. Read their accounts, ask each claimant a question, and take the object to the workbench. Then choose a recipient and stamp a receipt with the evidence that supports your decision.

Turn objects over and examine their parts. Lift the lid of a music box, line up a translucent test frame with a scratch inside a folding camera, or undo the clasps of a suitcase to see what lies beneath its tray. If a receipt is rejected, the bureau gives you a specific objection and keeps your draft so you can try again.

Work through twelve cases across four nights, with twenty-four fictional claimants and a letter for each completed case. Revisit filed receipts and explore the questions you left unasked. There’s no timer, and the game works offline.
