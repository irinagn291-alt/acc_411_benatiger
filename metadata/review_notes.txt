Benatiger is an offline, single-player deduction game. On a fresh launch, Rain · The Music Box opens directly. Ring the brass bell to reveal Eleanor Vale and Arthur Bell. Tap a claimant and ask one of the two written questions, then open Workbench and follow the inspection controls to lift the lid and draw out the signed shim. In Receipt, select Eleanor, attach the signed shim and press or drag the brass stamp. Letter shows the filed receipt and case letter. To test rejection before filing, attach the familiarity card alone and stamp. The game returns to the workbench with a specific objection and preserves the draft. The night board and archive let you resume available cases or revisit released ones.

The app supports iPhone and iPad. Progress and the sound preference are stored on-device. Audio and haptics are available on physical devices and disabled on simulators. The app requests no runtime permissions. The folding camera is an illustrated puzzle object and uses a bundled test frame. There are no accounts, server calls, analytics SDKs, adverts, purchases or cloud saves.
