Take the night shift at a lost and found. Two people claim each item, and it’s up to you to decide who gets it. Read their accounts, ask each claimant a question, and inspect the item at your workbench. Choose a recipient, attach the evidence that supports your decision, and stamp the receipt.

Turn items over and examine their parts. Lift the lid of a music box, line up a translucent test frame with a scratch inside a folding camera, or unfasten a suitcase to see what’s under its tray. If a receipt is rejected, you’ll get a specific objection and can revise the draft you already started.

Solve twelve cases over four nights, with twenty-four fictional claimants and a letter for every completed case. Return to the archive to reread filed receipts and explore the questions you didn’t ask. Play offline and take as much time as you need.
